UPDATE 1.3

Eight new mods, a cleaner client, and a stack of fixes. Here is everything that changed in this update.

New Content

What's New

Eight new mods have joined the pack, adding dark magic, ancient technology, dwarven engineering, and a new way to play as a monster instead of hunting one.

Malum

A mod about dark magic, built around a new arcane resource called Soul Fire and eerie, otherworldly structures to explore. Brings a whole new magic progression path into the world.

Added

Convenient Malum

A small quality of life addon for Malum that smooths out some of its more tedious mechanics. Installed alongside Malum to make the new magic system less of a chore to work with.

Added

Goety

Necromancy and dark rituals powered by souls. Raise the dead, bind spirits, and build out a whole gothic aesthetic with dozens of new blocks and decorations along the way.

Added

Embers Rekindled

A dwarven themed tech and magic mod centered on heat, fire, and forgework. A slower, more grounded alternative to the pack's other tech mods with its own machines and processing chains.

Added

Tetra

Modular tools and weapons that you build piece by piece and repair and improve over time instead of replacing. Also opens up ancient technology to find and restore while exploring.

Added

Vampirism

Become a vampire, or hunt them down as a vampire hunter. A full skill and progression system either way, with its own factions, villages, and late game vampire lord content.

Added

Werewolves

An addon for Vampirism that adds a second path: become a werewolf instead. Its own abilities, transformations, and progression, built to sit alongside the vampire side rather than replace it.

Added

Also included as supporting libraries, required by the mods above and invisible in play: Lodestone (used by Malum and Goety) and MUtil (used by Tetra).

Under The Hood

Fixes & Improvements

Alongside the new mods, this update cleans up a number of long standing issues with the client and server.

✓

Removed Oculus and Iris

These shaders mods had a rendering conflict with Malum's effects that could not be fixed while they were installed. Nothing else in the pack depended on them, so they have been removed entirely.

✓

Fixed Pixelmon interface bugs

Removed Pixelmon's extra buttons from the main menu and pause menu, and fixed a conflict where opening your inventory could sometimes pull up Pixelmon's screen instead of the normal one. The P key now reliably opens the Pixelmon party overlay.

✓

Removed telemetry and ad injection

Stripped MineTogether and the launcher's built in ad and telemetry injection from both the client and server. The pack no longer phones home to anyone it does not need to.

✓

Cleaned up leftover data from removed content

Player save data still referencing the old Rats mod, which was removed previously, has been cleaned up. This was only ever cosmetic log noise, but it is gone now.

✓

Rebound conflicting keybinds

The new mods introduced a few keybind collisions with each other and with existing keys. These have been rebound so every feature has its own key. See the table below.
