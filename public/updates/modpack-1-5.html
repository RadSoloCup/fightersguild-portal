Server Fixes, October 9

These changes are all on the server, so there is nothing to download. Pack 1.5.2 is still the current version.

Dungeons Content

Big Dungeons Spawn in One Piece

15 of the mod's biggest buildings, including the Mushroom Monstrosity's arena and the Endersent's lair, were too wide for the way the mod placed them, so they spawned chopped off at chunk edges. They now generate whole, with their bosses.

This applies to land nobody has explored yet. Buildings that already spawned cut off stay as they are.

World

New Land, Generated Ahead of You

A server bot called FG_Scout is flying over unexplored land and generating it ahead of time, out to about 4,500 blocks from spawn. Travelling somewhere new causes less lag, and Distant Horizons has more far terrain to show.

It stays well away from land that already exists, so your builds are never touched. If you see FG_Scout in the player list or chat, that is the bot at work. It also tests new land for crashes, which is how the Dungeons fix above was found.

Structures

Missing Pieces Put Back

Some structures were spawning with parts missing, because their building plans asked for pieces under the wrong name. As of the October 9 restart they spawn complete:

  • Strongholds: corridors, illager rooms and storage piles.
  • Dungeons Arise: the Mechanical Nest's decorations and the Foundry's gear corridors.
  • Dungeons Content villages: streets, villagers, animal pens, lamps and plants, plus a library chest that was always empty.
  • Vampirism hunter outposts get their missing tent, and Structory camps get their well bottoms.

Like the Dungeons fix, this applies to land generated from now on.

Major Changes

Under The Hood

Three changes in 1.5 are bigger than any single mod. They touch almost everything in the pack, so they get their own spotlight.

The Switch To Forge

NeoForge 47.1.84 → Forge 47.4.26

Since day one the pack has run on an early NeoForge build for Minecraft 1.20.1. Most 1.20.1 mods are now built and tested against the current Forge release instead, and the gap had grown wide enough that several of your suggestions simply would not start on our old loader. Update 1.5 moves the whole pack to Forge 47.4.26, the newest Forge for 1.20.1.

What this unlocks going forward

  • Current versions of the mods we already run. Apotheosis, Iron's Spells, Curios, Cyclic and others are on their latest 1.20.1 releases in this update, with all the fixes that came with them.
  • A much bigger pool for future suggestions. Mods that need a recent Forge, like Eternal Starlight, Bloodmoon Rebrushed and Smokeleaf Industry, are now possible.
  • The real fix for theater audio. The proper upstream fix for Waterframes audio cutting out needs a newer Forge than we had. That door is now open for a future patch.
  • Faster crash fixes. When a mod breaks, its authors fix it against current Forge. Being on the same version means we can take those fixes straight away instead of patching things ourselves.

New in Forge since our version

  • Faster, leaner loading. Mod scanning at startup is about 30% faster, the registries and capability system were optimised, and the event system was upgraded to a version with lower memory use and faster event handling (that is the code every mod uses to react to things in the game).
  • Crash reports that point at the culprit. Forge now analyses crash reports and names the mod most likely responsible, and mod loading errors and broken config files are reported clearly in the log instead of failing silently.
  • Keybinds with modifier keys work properly. Binds that use Shift, Ctrl or Alt now trigger correctly, even when several modifiers are held. Handy in a pack with this many keybinds.
  • Better command autocomplete. Typing a command now suggests modded items and blocks without needing the mod's name first.
  • Modded leaves follow your graphics setting. On Fast graphics, modded leaves now render like vanilla ones, which is easier on lower end PCs.
  • A sturdier loading screen. Several fixes for graphics drivers that had trouble with the early loading window, and a simpler memory readout while the game starts.

Major fixes

  • A missing optional mod no longer crashes the game through another mod's mixins.
  • Containers no longer close by themselves when your reach has been extended by gear or effects.
  • Blocks touched by lava no longer stay permanently flammable.
  • Fixed a networking bug that could drop the last packet while joining a server.
  • Fixed falling blocks not rendering while they fall, wrong boat travel distances, fire mobs ignoring freezing damage, and shields not being disabled by axe hits from some attackers.
  • Fixed a crash when an item breaks while it is being used, and memory use when checking entities in water and lava was reduced.

What you need to do

  • Nothing special. The Fighters Guild Launcher installs the new Forge automatically when it updates you to 1.5.
  • The first time you open your inventory after joining, the recipe viewer finishes building its index. Give it a few seconds before clicking around.

MineColonies, Fully Up To Date

MineColonies 1.1.603 → 1.1.1301

Our MineColonies was about seven hundred builds behind. 1.5 jumps all the way to the latest stable release, together with the libraries it is built on: Structurize, BlockUI and Domum Ornamentum.

What changes

  • Out with a crash-prone version. The version we ran took the server down on October 4 when a Barracks Tower finished upgrading. The colony code has been reworked many times since.
  • Smarter citizens. The old version logged hundreds of errors from citizens getting stuck on their way home, and occasionally duplicated citizens. The new version brings a long list of citizen and pathfinding fixes.
  • Hundreds of releases of fixes and improvements to buildings, citizen AI, builders and the colony interface.

New since our version

  • A full food rework. Over a dozen new crops (tomato, rice, onion, garlic, cabbage, soybean, eggplant and more) and a whole menu of new dishes from a reworked Cook and Restaurant. Citizen happiness now depends on the quality and variety of their food, and the simpler dishes can be crafted by players too.
  • Colony connections. Build a Gatehouse, lay a trail of the new sign blocks to another colony, and the two are linked. From there you can request an alliance, declare a feud, and travel between gatehouses.
  • Cavalry and new guard types. Stables let guards ride into battle on horseback, and two new guards join the barracks: the axe wielding Huscarl and the crossbow Marksman, whose hits partly ignore armor. Guards now also react when raiders attack a gate.
  • Raiders out in the world. Raider ships and camps now appear as structures in the world, with underwater raiders and tougher camp raiders.
  • Builder's Assistant Hammers. Gold, Iron and Diamond hammers let you jump in and place blocks on an active build site yourself instead of waiting for your builder.
  • A colony map item showing citizen complaints, sick and hungry citizens, guard tower ranges, and a prestige high score of colonies ranked by how impressive their buildings are.
  • Leisure time. Citizens now take short breaks to visit the library, university and town hall, with icons over their heads showing when they are idle, sleeping or mourning.
  • Statistics pages for every building, so you can see what each worker has actually produced.
  • New job skills: the Cowhand can now herd and milk goats, the Lumberjack has a craft-only mode, a new research lets crops spread sideways, and Postbox minimum stock keeps you topped up automatically.
  • The Ancient Athens style, new walls and gatehouses in several styles, and a big pass over almost every existing style.
  • Compatibility with Waystones and Productive Bees, and every research can now be undone.

Major fixes

  • Pathfinding was rebuilt several times over: citizens handle ladders, bridges, water and steep drops far better and get unstuck on their own.
  • The request system and warehouse were made much more resilient, fixing builders asking for items they already had and requests getting stuck.
  • Citizen duplication prevention, and fixes for citizens loaded from chunks replacing active ones.
  • Fixed guard patrols piling up events and causing lag, plus a large speedup for colony rendering and citizen path following.
  • Removing a mod no longer crashes or corrupts a colony that used its food, jobs or recipes.
  • Dozens of crash fixes, including racks next to huts, modded armor on citizens, and colonies reaching into unloaded chunks.

Good to know

  • Colony data is upgraded the first time the 1.5 world loads. That upgrade only goes one way, which is one of the reasons we take a full snapshot first (see the launch plan below).
  • MineColonies' recipe pages in the recipe viewer are much bigger now, so the first inventory open after joining takes a little longer.

Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, Big Upgrade

Iron's Spells 3.4.0.7 → 3.16.3

Iron's Spells gets its largest update since it joined the pack: a long list of new spells, items and balance changes, plus a rebuilt recipe viewer integration. The old version did not scale with all the new weapons in 1.5 and froze the game while loading, which the new version fixes.

Iron's Spells 3.15 brought every new feature from versions 3.5 through 3.15 back to Minecraft 1.20.1 in one go, so we skip straight past twelve versions of content. It now needs a small helper library, Iron's Lib, which comes with the update automatically.

New bosses and places

  • The Citadel, a new structure guarded by Citadel Keepers, and Tyros, the fire boss at its heart.
  • Ice Spider Dens with the new Ice Spider mob.
  • The Dead King returns. Use a Phylactery to bring him back, or face the new Ominous Dead King fight.
  • New vault variants for trial loot and custom boss health bars.

19 new spells

  • Sunbeam, Cleanse, Ice Spikes, Fire Arrow, Raise Hell, Summon Swords, Touch Dig, Volt Strike, Ice Tomb, Snowball, Frostbite, Pocket Dimension, Shadow Slash, Throw, Fang Swirl, Blizzard, Arcane Shackle, Gravity Fissure and Scapegoat.

New gear

  • Armor: the Wizard set, the Infernal Sorcerer's and Lightbringer's chestplates, and Boots of Speed.
  • Weapons and staves: Boreal Blade, Twilight Gale, Hellrazor, Decrepit Scythe, Legionnaire Flameberge and the Pyrium Staff.
  • Trinkets and books: Signet of the Betrayer, Greater Conjurer's Talisman, Amulet of Teleportation, and the Cursed Doll and Frostbranded spellbooks.
  • Blocks and music: the Portal Frame, Wisewood Bookshelf and Brazier, plus three new music discs.

Reworked systems

  • The Alchemist Cauldron now works with fluids.
  • Summons were reworked and can be recast to refresh them.
  • Late game armor is now crafted on the Smithing Table using runes.
  • A toggle option for the spell wheel keybind, and wizard merchants now trade school runes.

Major fixes

  • The recipe viewer integration no longer burns CPU building recipes, the cause of our freeze while loading.
  • Spells now respect PvP and friendly fire rules properly.
  • Fixed summons getting out of sync between the server and players.
  • Many balance passes across spells, gear and mobs.

Polish

Fixes and Tuning

Things we found and fixed while testing 1.5.

Performance

Smoother frame rates around busy bases

  • Souls from Dungeons Content that nobody collects now fade after a minute. They were piling up near mob farms and slowing the game for everyone nearby. Souls from your own fights still fly to you and fill your soul meter as usual.
  • Productive Bees centrifuges no longer draw the combs spinning inside them, a big frame rate win around large apiaries. The centrifuges work exactly the same.
Balance

Balancing changes

A few items and item combinations turned out to be far stronger than intended once the new mods were in the mix, and have been adjusted.

Seasonal

Trick or Trainer, minus the hordes

Already Live

What We Shipped Since Last Weekend

A lot landed on the server, the launcher and this site over the past few days. All of this went live ahead of 1.5.

Website

The Server Map

The new Map tab shows a full, zoomable map of our world, rendered straight from the server. It opens on the Overworld in a flat, top-down view, and you can switch to 3D or to other dimensions. More of the map fills in with each render.

Launcher and Website

33 Shader Packs, One Click

The Fighters Guild Launcher has a new Shaders button. Pick any of 33 shader packs, sorted into light, medium and heavy, and the launcher installs them along with everything they need. Remove any of them just as easily.

Every pack is listed with a screenshot and description in the new Shaders tab.

Website

Leaderboards

The new Leaderboard tab ranks everyone by play time, kills, deaths, blocks mined and distance traveled, using the same stats as !mc stats in chat. It refreshes every week.

Launcher

A Launcher That Updates Itself

  • Launcher 1.0.5 checks for new versions and updates itself automatically.
  • Your Minecraft Launcher profile now uses the Fighters Guild crossed swords icon.
  • Smoother memory settings by default, which keep Distant Horizons from stuttering.
Server

A Huge Performance Boost

We tracked down something that had been keeping around 560 chunks loaded and running around the clock, even with nobody online. With that fixed, the server does about a third of the work it used to when idle.

The server also got more memory and better tuning. During play, lag warnings dropped from about 150 an hour to nearly none.

World

A Fresh Nether and Bigger Biomes

The Nether was wiped and regenerated from a brand new seed. Newly explored Overworld land now generates with Large Biomes, so there is more room to roam between landmarks.

Distant Horizons shows the far landscape on both the server and your client, generated by the server so your PC does not have to.

New in 1.5

Caves Are Back

A small bug in one mod's world data was stopping cave and ravine carving in newly generated land. 1.5 includes the fix, so fresh terrain gets its caves and ravines again, along with the new cave biomes from Alex's Caves.

New in 1.5

Distant Horizons, Focused

In 1.5, Distant Horizons only draws far terrain in the Overworld, the Nether, the End and the second Overworld. Skipping the many side dimensions saves server resources for the worlds people actually explore.

Community Suggestions

Where Your Suggestions Landed

Every mod you suggested for 1.5, and what happened to it. Some made it in, some made it in with a few quirks to know about, some got a replacement, and a few could not be added. Here is why.

Bloodmoon Added

Added as Bloodmoon Rebrushed. Blood moon nights spawn far more monsters, so expect more lag than usual while one is up, especially with several players spread out. Enhanced Celestials also has a blood moon, which we switched off so the two never stack.

Stellarity Added

Stellarity only reshapes End terrain that has not been generated yet. Parts of the End we have already explored stay as they are, so you may see sharp borders where old and new terrain meet. Head further out for the full experience.

Memories of the Past Not added

We added and tested it, but its leveling and stat system overlapped too much with the progression the pack already has, so characters stacked bonuses from several systems at once. It was pulled before release.

Knight Quest Added

Its armor and mobs are detailed animated models. On lower end PCs, crowds of them or lots of armored players can cost some frame rate.

Eternal Starlight Added

Eternal Starlight is officially made for newer Minecraft versions. We are using a community port to 1.20.1, so a few items or features may be rough or missing compared to the original. Distant Horizons does not draw far terrain in its dimension.

Terra Entity Added

In testing, its NPC traders sometimes failed to refresh their trades and logged an error. It does not crash the game, but a trader may show stale or missing trades.

Bosses of Mass Destruction Added

It and the Aether both change the boss health bar at the top of the screen, so the bars may overlap if you fight bosses from both at once. Boss fights are heavy, so expect a bit of lag during big fights.

Smokeleaf Industry Added

Its recipes do not show up in the recipe viewer. Smokeleaf needs a newer recipe viewer than we can run, because the newer one froze the game for minutes while loading with a pack this size. The mod itself works normally.

P

Scape and Run: Parasites Not added

Scape and Run: Parasites only exists for Minecraft 1.12.2, years older than our version. We tested Fungal Infection: Spore as a stand-in, since it has the same spreading infection idea, but it took over too much of the world for a shared survival server, so it was left out as well.

Dragon Mounts Remastered Replaced

Remastered is only available for Minecraft 1.21.1. It is replaced by Dragon Mounts: Legacy, which lets you hatch, raise and ride dragons on our version. Legacy is still marked beta, so report anything odd.

VR

Vivecraft Not in 1.5

Vivecraft rewrites how the game draws the world and handles input, and those same areas are already customized by FancyMenu, Embeddium, Distant Horizons and the shader packs. Mixing all of that safely needs its own round of testing. We may revisit it later as an optional add-on, like shaders.

🎮

Controlify Not in 1.5

Controlify has no official version for our setup, only an unofficial port. It reworks every menu and input in the game, the same parts FancyMenu, Controlling and the recipe viewer already change, so it needs dedicated testing first. We may revisit it later.

E

Etherology Not available

Etherology only exists for Minecraft 1.21 on the Fabric loader. There is no version that can run in our pack, and no close replacement.

AoA

Advent of Ascension Not added

Its 1.20.1 version is unfinished. The mod itself warns that all of its dimensions and most of its mobs are missing on this version. At 140 MB it also added a lot of memory use and loading time for very little content, so we pulled it after testing.

Launch Plan

How 1.5 Goes Live

This is the biggest change the pack has ever had, so we are rolling it out carefully.

Snapshot first, then a 48 hour test

  1. Before anything changes, we take a full snapshot of the server on 1.4: the world, every colony, every base and all the configs.
  2. 1.5 goes live with a 48 hour test period. Play normally, and report crashes, broken items, missing blocks or anything that feels off in the Discord.
  3. We fix what we can on the spot. Most issues in a big update are config tweaks or a mod version away from a fix.
  4. If something cannot be overcome, we fully revert the server to the 1.4 snapshot. Anything built or collected during the test period would be lost in that case, so keep that in mind before committing big projects in those two days.
